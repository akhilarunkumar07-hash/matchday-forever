Power shot hold shoot until the bar turns green and it goes in, every time. Camera V. PS5 controller plug it in and press any button: left stick moves; with the ball Cross pass, Circle shoot (hold to charge), Square lob, Triangle through ball; without it Cross or L1 switches player, Square tackles, Circle slides; R1 or R2 sprint, R3 camera, Options pause. In the menu use the d-pad: left/right picks your team, up/down the opponent, Cross kicks off. Pause P. A slide wins the ball more often, but a late one from behind is a foul, and fouls bring free kicks, penalties and cards. On a phone, turn it sideways and use the on-screen stick and buttons.

FutBasket a 3D street court where football meets basketball: 2 v 2, 2 minutes, most points wins. Run into the ball to dribble it. Click while dribbling to flick it into the air, then click again when it is near you to volley it at the rival hoop (a bicycle kick). Baskets count 2 points, or 3 from beyond the yellow arc. Rivals slide-tackle (you stumble for 1 s and drop the ball) and jump to head shots away. Keys A / D or arrows run, W or Space jump, S or Shift slide, click or K flick and volley, Q swap player. PS5: stick or d-pad run, Cross jump, Square flick and volley, Circle slide, L1 swap.
